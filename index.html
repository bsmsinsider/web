About BSMS Insider

BSMS Insider is an independent, student-run digital news network dedicated to bringing the latest updates directly to the student body of Boiling Springs Middle School.

Founded with the goal of connecting students, our team covers everything from pep rallies and athletic events to club activities and daily announcements. We believe in providing a voice for the students, by the students.
